# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (template)

> **Have a lawyer review this before you send it.** This is a working template
> written to be short and genuinely mutual so it can be signed the day it is
> asked for. It is not legal advice, and it is not adapted to any particular
> jurisdiction. Fill the bracketed fields, set the governing law to your own,
> and get it reviewed once — after that it is reusable for every partner.
>
> Keep a signed PDF ready to send. The moment to send an NDA is the moment
> someone asks, not three days later.

---

**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on **[DATE]** between:

**[HARBINGER LABS LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a company registered in
**[JURISDICTION]** with registered address **[ADDRESS]** ("**Party A**"); and

**[COUNTERPARTY LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a company registered in
**[JURISDICTION]** with registered address **[ADDRESS]** ("**Party B**").

Each may be referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**".

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship concerning the
evaluation and possible use of Party A's Active Directory attack-path analysis
software (the "**Purpose**"). In doing so, each Party may disclose confidential
information to the other. This Agreement governs that exchange in both
directions equally.

### 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by one
Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**") in connection with
the Purpose, in any form, whether or not marked as confidential, that a
reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the
circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation:

(a) for Party A: software, source code, algorithms, models, model outputs,
    methodology, roadmap, pricing, and unreleased product plans;

(b) for Party B: network architecture, directory structure, security posture,
    identified vulnerabilities and attack paths, client identities, commercial
    terms, and any output generated by the software when run against Party B's
    or its clients' environments.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can
demonstrate: (a) was publicly available at the time of disclosure, or became so
through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in the Recipient's
possession without a duty of confidence before disclosure; (c) was lawfully
received from a third party without a duty of confidence; or (d) was
independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the
Discloser's Confidential Information.

### 4. Obligations

The Recipient shall:

(a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;

(b) not disclose it to any third party except to its employees, officers, and
    professional advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by
    confidentiality obligations no less protective than these;

(c) protect it using at least the same degree of care it applies to its own
    confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than a
    reasonable degree of care; and

(d) not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software or object code
    disclosed under this Agreement, except to the extent that restriction is
    unenforceable under applicable law.

### 5. Compelled disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by
law, regulation, or a court or regulatory authority of competent jurisdiction,
provided it gives the Discloser prompt written notice (where lawful to do so)
and reasonable assistance in seeking protective treatment, and discloses only
the portion legally required.

### 6. No transfer of rights

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in
this Agreement grants any licence, right, title, or interest in any intellectual
property, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise.

### 7. No obligation to proceed

Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to disclose any information,
enter into any further agreement, or proceed with any transaction.

### 8. Term and survival

This Agreement takes effect on the date above and continues for **two (2)
years**. The confidentiality obligations in clause 4 survive for **three (3)
years** from the date of each disclosure. Confidential Information that
constitutes a trade secret remains protected for as long as it qualifies as a
trade secret under applicable law.

### 9. Return or destruction

On the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall promptly return or
destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, and confirm this in
writing. The Recipient may retain: (a) one archival copy solely for the purpose
of demonstrating compliance with this Agreement; and (b) copies held in routine
automated backups, which remain subject to this Agreement until deleted in the
ordinary course.

### 10. No warranty

All Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party makes any
warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

### 11. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause harm for which
damages alone are an inadequate remedy, and that the Discloser may seek
injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy available.

### 12. Data protection

Where either Party processes personal data of the other in connection with the
Purpose, it shall do so in accordance with applicable data protection law. The
Parties acknowledge that Party A's software processes data **locally on Party
B's systems and transmits no directory data to Party A**, and that Party A
therefore does not expect to act as a processor of Party B's directory data.

### 13. General

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on this subject and
supersedes all prior discussions. It may be amended only in writing signed by
both Parties. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in
force. Neither Party may assign it without the other's written consent, except
to a successor of all or substantially all of its business. It may be executed
in counterparts, including electronically.

### 14. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[JURISDICTION]**, and the Parties
submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[JURISDICTION]**.

## Practical notes

- **Send it the same day it is asked for.** Delay reads as unpreparedness, and
  the request usually comes at the exact moment interest is highest.
- **Clause 12 is the one they will read.** It states plainly that their
  directory data never reaches you, which is usually the actual worry behind the
  NDA request. Keep it accurate — it is only true while the tool stays offline
  by default.
- **Do not negotiate hard on a mutual NDA.** It costs you nothing and buys
  goodwill. If their legal team sends their own paper instead, sign theirs
  unless something is genuinely unusual.
- If you use e-signature, have the template loaded and ready to send in two
  clicks.
