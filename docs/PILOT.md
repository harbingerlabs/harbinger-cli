# Design partner pilot — the shape

**Free through the design-partner phase, in exchange for feedback and a
reference. Not free forever, and not open-ended.**

Open-ended free never converts. It creates no urgency, no decision point, and no
moment where anyone has to say yes or no. This document is the defined shape, so
that both sides know what they are agreeing to and when it ends.

---

## What you get

- The full product, unrestricted, for the pilot period. Not a trial tier.
- Direct access to the person who builds it — not a support queue.
- Anything you need that is a day of work and makes the tool better gets built
  during the pilot, ahead of the roadmap.
- Your pricing at conversion is locked to the design-partner rate for
  **24 months**, whatever the list price becomes.

## What we ask

Three things, all cheap, all specific:

1. **Run it on a real directory.** Not a lab. The whole point is contact with
   twenty years of accumulated permissions.
2. **One structured feedback session** at the end of the pilot — 30 minutes,
   against [FEEDBACK.md](FEEDBACK.md). Plus the one-question form after your
   first run, which takes under a minute.
3. **A reference.** Either a named quote we can use, or a 20-minute call with
   one prospect of your choosing. You approve the wording; you can decline any
   individual prospect.

If the tool turns out not to be useful, say so and we part cleanly. A clear no
is worth more than a polite maybe, and we will not chase.

## Duration

**Six weeks from your first successful run**, extendable once by two weeks if
your schedule slips. That is long enough for at least two collections and a
diff, which is where the tool actually earns its keep.

At the end there is a single decision point: convert, or stop. Not "keep using
it while we think about it."

## Cost

Nothing during the pilot. No card, no invoice, no auto-conversion. If you do
nothing at the end, it simply stops — you will never wake up to a charge you did
not agree to.

## Number of partners

**Capped at six.** This is not artificial scarcity; it is the number whose
feedback one person can act on properly. When it is full, it is full.

---

## What we do with your data

Nothing. The tool runs entirely on your machine and transmits nothing — see
[DATA_HANDLING.md](DATA_HANDLING.md). We never see your directory, your export,
or your report. The feedback you send us is what you choose to type into a form.

If you want an NDA before any of this, [MUTUAL_NDA.md](MUTUAL_NDA.md) is ready
to send — mutual, short, and we sign it the day you ask.

---

## What happens after

At conversion:

- Pricing is per-directory-under-management, published, not negotiated per deal.
- Your design-partner rate holds for 24 months.
- The free tier remains genuinely free and complete for a single snapshot
  analysis. It is not crippleware, and you are not being moved onto a worse
  version of something you already had.

---

## The honest version

We need a small number of MSPs to tell us whether ranked attack paths change
what they actually do, or just produce another report nobody schedules work
against. That is the question. If the answer is no, we would rather find out
from six people in six weeks than from sixty in a year.
